# Student Performance Tracker and Academic Risk Predictor

## Problem Statement

Students may find it difficult to understand their academic performance from individual subject marks. Low marks and poor attendance can increase the risk of failing, but students may not notice this early. This project provides a simple Python-based system to calculate student performance and identify academic risk.

## Scope of the Project

The project accepts a student's name, attendance percentage, and marks for Maths, Science, and English. It calculates total marks, average marks, grade, and risk status. It also gives a simple suggestion based on the student's result.

## Target Users

- Students who want to check their academic performance
- Teachers who want a quick view of student progress

## High-Level Features

1. Accept student name, attendance, and subject marks.
2. Calculate total marks and average marks.
3. Assign a grade based on the average.
4. Show Low Risk, Medium Risk, or High Risk based on marks and attendance.
5. Give a study suggestion to the student.
6. Validate incorrect input, such as marks below 0, marks above 100, or empty names.

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
